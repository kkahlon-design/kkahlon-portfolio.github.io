A bit about me

I'm a UX designer who cares about the small moments where people get stuck.

Working in software support, I watched real customers use the products I now design. It taught me that most problems are hiding in plain sight.

How I think about design

  • Approach
  • Process

01

Human-centred from the start. I design for the person using it, not the person presenting it.

Empathise. Define. Ideate. Prototype. Test. Not a checklist, a mindset.

Inspired by Dieter Rams and Apple's attention to detail: work that feels effortless to use, but is carefully considered in every detail.

Nothing is ever finished. There's always something to improve, refine or rethink. That's not a flaw in the process. It is the process.
